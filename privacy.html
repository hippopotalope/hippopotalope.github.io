Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Socials is used only by its owner. With the owner's permission it reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive folder for their videos. It does not collect data from anyone else, does not sell or share any data, and does not use Google data for advertising or to train AI models. Google user data is used only to move the owner's own files, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
